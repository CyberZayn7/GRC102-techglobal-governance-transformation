# GRC102: TechGlobal Governance Transformation Simulation

## Overview

This project presents a comprehensive information security governance transformation initiative for TechGlobal, a multinational organization with 2,500 employees across five global offices.

The report addresses governance gaps, stakeholder accountability, committee structures, risk escalation processes, segregation of duties controls, and governance artefacts required to establish a mature cybersecurity governance program.

## Objectives

- Assess governance weaknesses and business impacts
- Design a robust security governance structure
- Define stakeholder responsibilities and authority
- Establish governance committees and escalation mechanisms
- Develop a RACI accountability framework
- Implement cyber-risk escalation thresholds
- Address segregation-of-duties conflicts

## Key Deliverables

### Governance Gap Assessment
Identified critical governance weaknesses including:
- Centralized decision-making
- Lack of governance committees
- Inconsistent business unit security practices
- Limited executive visibility
- Informal risk escalation paths

### Stakeholder Governance Framework
Defined responsibilities for:
- Board of Directors
- CEO
- CISO
- CRO
- Legal & Compliance
- Finance
- Human Resources
- IT Operations
- Business Units

### Governance Committee Ecosystem
- Executive Security Council
- Security Governance Steering Committee
- Specialized Working Groups

### RACI Accountability Matrix
Mapped ownership and accountability across critical security governance activities.

### Cyber-Risk Escalation Framework
Defined:
- Operational escalation
- Executive escalation
- Board-level escalation

### Segregation of Duties Controls
Identified governance conflicts and implemented mitigation recommendations.
